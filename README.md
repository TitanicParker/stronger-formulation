# Stronger Formulation

This repository is not an archive. It is a working case architecture.

## The controlling mechanism

> **The founding synthesis did not merely record uncertainty. It organised the uncertainty.**

The patient entered Neurology with painful feet, abnormal gait, increased tone/stiffness, movement difficulty and toe clawing still inside one open clinical problem.

Neurology identified severe Parkinsonism and a dystonic-looking lower limb.

The later specialist synthesis then allowed the foot/leg symptoms to be **“completely separate”**, possibly neuropathic, and **“Not clear.”**

That may have changed the information state for every later clinician.

There is a difference between inheriting:

> **We have not established how the neurological disorder and the painful feet relate.**

and inheriting:

> **Parkinsonism is established; the foot problem may be separate; its cause is not clear.**

The second formulation can make later local explanations accumulate without forcing reconstruction of the original relationship.

> **The problem is not that information disappeared. The problem is the shape in which it travelled.**

That is the mechanism the repository tests.

## The centre

> **Unresolved source of disabling pain**  
> **becomes specialist uncertainty about a possibly separate foot problem.**

Then, by 2023:

> **the same unresolved pain becomes a safety problem while the explanatory model governing it is expressly recognised as misaligned.**

## The three arguments

### 1. Founding separation

> **They saw the abnormal neurological walk. They knew the feet hurt. What work earned the separation?**

This argument requires no Protest.

### 2. Protest fork

If the patient proves that he said, in substance, **“You have not dealt with my feet,”** the question changes from possible omission to decision after notice.

> **The Protest is the widest point in the future.**

It exposes the clinical and documentary choices that remained open while the patient was still there.

### 3. Safety and misalignment

By 2023, without relying on the Protest, the documentary record contains direct life-tolerability language, an urgent GP **“death wish”** warning, **“I am worried about him,”** nursing **“no quality of life,”** and an express neurological record of **“misalignment.”**

The question becomes:

> **What did reasonable care require once the pain had become a safety problem while the explanatory model itself was visibly contested?**

## The longitudinal test

At every transition ask:

> **Did the new evidence merely receive another local label, or did it require reconstruction of the original feet–gait–movement–pain relationship?**

## Repository rule

Every file must have a unique job.

No duplicate summaries. No historical versions kept for sentiment. No “strongest points” files that merely restate the architecture. No old essay retained as an archive.

A document stays only if it does one of five things:

- proves facts;
- organises the three arguments;
- teaches the case;
- asks an expert a necessary question;
- controls evidential discipline.

Everything else goes.

## Drafting rule

> **Quote first. Fact second. Question third. Medicine fourth. Law last.**

Do not overclaim. Structural pathology was real. Podiatry was appropriate. Later reassessment occurred. Documentary absence is not proof of non-occurrence. The February 2023 email is not itself proof of suicidal intent. The 2023 misalignment and “embarrassing” passages are not proof of bad faith.

The case is stronger without claims it does not need.
