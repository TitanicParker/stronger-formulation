# Meet the Team

## Expertise, decisions, information states and behaviour states

**Function:** Identify who possessed which information, who participated in which decisions, and how the founding specialist allocation affected each actor and institutional function across the life of the record.

It is not a guilt map.

The controlling distinctions are:

> **Capability is not participation, and participation is not bedside application.**

> **The summary changes knowledge downstream; it changes commitment upstream.**

> **Clinical inheritance is not the same thing as governance adoption.**

> **Responsibility follows information opportunity.**

---

# 1. The state model

## Presentation state

The patient reaches Neurology as one integrated clinical problem: bilateral painful abnormal feet, abnormal gait, increased tone / stiffness, movement-initiation difficulty, toe clawing / deformity and deteriorating function.

## Bedside-detection state

Neurology detects severe Parkinsonism, a strikingly abnormal gait, a foot-drop-like lower-limb phenomenon considered dystonic rather than true foot drop, pes planus and hammertoes.

The question is not merely who saw the sign.

> **Who converted the detected components into a directed investigation of their relationship before separation?**

## Allocation state

The final synthesis says the foot / leg symptoms:

> **“may have been completely separate”**

records that the patient had:

> **“in his own mind - markedly divorced”**

his bodily / leg tension from corns, callouses and foot pains, and leaves the relationship:

> **“Not clear.”**

with Podiatry as the initial foot pathway.

## Reopening state

A later actor may acquire independent information capable of testing the founding allocation. The issue becomes what that person or function did with the new information.

# 2. The explicit examination-gap test

A conservative complete-record audit found **20 examination / question domains for which no adequate documented counterpart was identified**:

- foot pain at rest;
- great-toe extension;
- ankle twisting / dystonic torsion;
- foot posture while sitting;
- backward walking;
- heel walking;
- toe walking;
- tandem walking;
- heel strike;
- Achilles reflex;
- true ankle-clonus test;
- plantar response / Babinski;
- formal light-touch foot sensation;
- formal pinprick foot sensation;
- vibration at the great toe;
- joint-position / proprioception;
- capillary refill / temperature / colour;
- focal bony tenderness;
- plantar-fascia tenderness;
- Achilles-tendon tenderness.

This is not a claim that all 20 were mandatory or not performed undocumented.

Its actor-level function is narrower:

> **Where a clinician or service advances “complete separation” or peripheral neuropathy as explanatory possibilities, what examination evidence actually supports those alternatives?**

Do not count as missing things documented later. In 2020, for example, Neurology expressly documents normal dorsiflexion, plantarflexion, inversion and eversion power. Podiatry later provides detailed pressure / loading data. The negative audit is deliberately limited to explicit absences.

# 3. The expertise test

Every expertise claim must be run through:

> **Capability → Participation → Bedside Application**

The return-loop question remains:

> **Did the expertise that enriched the theory return to the body?**

The PRKN timing fork is now central:

> **If recessive / PRKN-type Parkinsonism was already operative while the patient remained available, where is the corresponding phenotype-directed examination of the painful feet and dystonic-looking lower limb?**

If that theory crystallised later:

> **Where is the return to the patient to test the improved theory against the unresolved feet?**

---

# 4. The Protest

The Protest is treated as fact:

> **You have not dealt with my feet.**

It does not mark the earliest possible breach.

> **It converts a pre-existing possible omission into express notice while the patient remains physically available.**

# 6. Aaron Brennan — input comparator and downstream recipient

Brennan sent:

> **feet + gait + tone + movement initiation + clawing + deteriorating function.**

He did not witness the inpatient neurological work.

He later received a strongly formulated Parkinsonian syndrome together with possible complete separation, possible neuropathy, “Not clear” and Podiatry.

He could not tell whether the residual uncertainty was **earned after adequate examination** or whether the necessary discriminating work had not been completed.

> **An insufficiency of examination may have been transmitted as an insufficiency of medical knowledge.**

In March 2019 Brennan again explicitly links feet, increased tone, abnormal gait, Parkinson’s disease, clawing and pain. That makes him a key re-integration actor at the point active Podiatry ends.

---

# 7. Thomas Monaghan — founding encounter, allocation and commitment

Monaghan possessed or had access to the referral, patient, severe Parkinsonian phenotype, dystonic-looking lower limb, painful foot history, structural foot findings, admission course, Protest and synthesis process.

For him the final summary is not merely information.

> **The summary changes knowledge downstream; it changes commitment upstream.**

The Monaghan questions are now:

1. **What relationship-directed foot examination was done before separation?**
2. **Which of the explicitly undocumented discriminating domains would reasonable practice actually have required in this presentation?**
3. **What positive examination evidence supported peripheral neuropathy?**
4. **What clinical evidence justified “may have been completely separate”?**
5. **Did “markedly divorced” describe phenomenological difference or justify mechanistic independence?**
6. **If PRKN / recessive Parkinsonism was already operative, where is the phenotype-directed foot examination?**
7. **If it crystallised later, why is there no demonstrated return to test the improved formulation against the feet?**
8. **What should the Protest have changed?**
9. **What should the October 2017 / April 2018 treatment-state crossings have changed?**

No allegation of motive or bad faith is required.

> **When did Counihan’s expertise enter the patient-specific case, what information did he receive, and did it produce bedside examination capable of distinguishing structural independence, neurological influence, neuropathy or mixed mechanism?**

# 10. Daniel Healy — independent specialist state

Healy did not possess the July bedside encounter. His September review generated an independent later state: probable PARK II and substantial treatment response.

His authority should not be treated as independent validation of a foot relationship he was never asked to assess.

> **What exactly did Galway ask Healy to decide, and did that scope include the painful feet–dystonia–gait relationship?**

# 11. Podiatry — mechanical archive and operational owner until March 2019

Podiatry documents recurrent fifth-MTPJ pathology, clawed toes, tight gastroc / soleus, pronating foot type, debridement, offloading, toe props and insoles.

It establishes genuine structural / mechanical disease.

The questions are:

> **What neurological question was Podiatry asked?**

> **What mechanism existed for its loading findings to return to Neurology?**

> **Who owned the arrows while both services were active?**

The available record identifies **25 March 2019 as the last Podiatry attendance**.

---

# 12. Galway Neurology after March 2019 — direct reacquisition

After March 2019, active Podiatry ownership is no longer shown in the available record.

Neurology itself later records Parkinsonian foot throw, excessive movement, exquisite sensitivity, morning warm-up, possible OFF dystonia, OFF-related pain / shuffling, toe curling and dopaminergic improvement in foot pain.

The institutional question changes:

> **Once the feet were no longer elsewhere, what continued to keep the founding separation alive?**

---

# 13. QPS / PAR / SIMT — governance adoption

By 2025 the historical integration issue is expressly placed before hospital governance.

The matter proceeds through QPS and later IMF / PAR / SIMT review. The record describes the SIMT result as:

> **“no deficit in care.”**

In January 2026 QPS maintains that the completed governance processes remain valid despite later dystonia information and that reopening / alteration / qualification is not required.

This produces a distinct state:

> **clinical inheritance → institutional review → governance adoption / continued reliance.**

The governing question is:

> **Did the review independently reconstruct the founding examination and ask what work earned the separation, or principally evaluate the completed record through the architecture it inherited?**

That is an institutional-analysis question, not proof of governance breach.

---

# 14. St Vincent’s — later independent comparator

The primary December 2025 dystonia letter remains an evidential gap in the reviewed corpus.

The February 2026 genetic-results letter directly confirms two pathogenic Parkin variants and states that the patient's:

> **Parkinsonism and dystonia are due to compound heterozygous mutations in the Parkin gene.**

This does not prove the historic foot pain was all dystonia and cannot be used retrospectively to create 2017 knowledge.
