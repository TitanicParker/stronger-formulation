Stronger Formulation

One case.
Three arguments.

Unresolved source of disabling pain becomes specialist uncertainty about a possibly separate foot problem.

Then, by 2023, the same unresolved pain becomes a safety problem while the explanatory model governing it is expressly recognised as misaligned.

The controlling mechanism

The founding synthesis did not merely record uncertainty. It organised the uncertainty.

The original problem entered Neurology as an open relationship between painful feet, abnormal gait, increased tone and movement difficulty. The later specialist synthesis allowed the feet to be “completely separate”, possibly neuropathic, and “Not clear”.

That may have changed what every later clinician inherited. There is a difference between “we have not established how these problems relate” and “Parkinsonism is established; the feet may be separate; the cause is not clear.”

The problem is not that information disappeared. The problem is the shape in which it travelled.

From then on, new findings can be explained locally — structure, neuropathy, OFF pain, dystonia, medication effect — without necessarily rebuilding the founding feet–gait–movement–pain relationship.

The longitudinal test is simple:

Did the new evidence merely receive another local label, or did it require reconstruction of the original problem?

The teaching test

Explain the case in six moves: what entered Neurology; what Neurology found; what happened to the pain question; what information state the next clinician inherited; what changes if the Protest is true; and what 2023 independently proves.

Then ask: What case do you think I should take?

The answer is not evidence. It tests whether the architecture teaches the real evidential choice cleanly enough for a non-specialist to see it.
